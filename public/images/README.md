Image slots for the invitation
==============================

Replace or add these files when the final photos are ready:

- intro-background.jpg: full first-screen background, portrait crop.
- photo-left.jpg: tall arched couple photo behind the envelope.
- photo-polaroid.jpg: small square/landscape photo in the paper frame.
- flowers-right.png: transparent bouquet/flower cluster for the right side.
- footer-photo.jpg: closing image at the bottom.

Recommended source sizes:

- Portrait/fullscreen images: at least 930 x 1700 px.
- Small framed photos: at least 900 px on the shortest side.
- Transparent bouquet PNG: around 900 x 1300 px.

The page already references these exact paths, so adding files with these names will replace the visible placeholders automatically.
